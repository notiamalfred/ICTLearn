Barcodes & QR Codes

One-dimensional and two-dimensional codes for storing text strings.

Barcodes QR Codes Fault Tolerance

Barcodes

What is a barcode?

Barcode: codes that consist of bars and spaces of different widths to represent a text string. This string is often a sequence of numbers representing a product ID.

Elements of a barcode

ElementFunction
Barcode symbolIt is the main element of a barcode. When scanned, it can be decoded to a sequence of characters.
Quiet zoneIt is the empty margin at each end of the barcode symbol. If the quiet zone is too small, it will be hard to scan the barcode symbol.
Start character and end characterThey indicate the start and the end of the barcode symbol respectively.
Text string represented by the barcodeIt is optional and for humans to read. Scanners will not read these characters.

Advantage

Compared with manually typing the numbers, barcode scanning reduces typing errors and input time.

A readable barcode must:

  • Have sufficient space for the quiet zone
  • Keep the widths of its bars and spaces unchanged

QR Codes

What is a QR code?

Quick Response code (QR code): made up of numerous black dots arranged in a white square background. It represents a text string which is usually a Uniform Resource Locator (URL), a phone number or an email address.

ElementFunction
Quiet zoneQR code has a quiet zone around the four sides.
Position patternThey are located at three of the four corners in a QR code. They allow the QR code to be scanned from any direction.
Alignment patternWhen the scanner is at an angle, the QR code will look distorted. By referring to the alignment pattern, the QR scanner can adjust the scanned image and fix the distortion.

Fault Tolerance

Error correction in QR codes

QR codes contain error-correction data, so they can still be decoded when rotated, distorted, dirty or partly covered.
